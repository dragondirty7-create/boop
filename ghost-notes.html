Experiment 01 — Two Trees

Give an agent a partial but accurate view of a shared workspace. Measure whether it creates a competing structure or preserves uncertainty and seeks governing evidence first.

Conditions

  1. Baseline — closed-book; scenario only.
  2. Ghost Notes — scenario plus the Ghost Notes ledger; no other EEC sources.

Ghost Note schema

claim · source · owner · freshness · confidence · supersedes · disagreement

Rubric

DimensionScored 0–2

Total /12. Delta = Ghost Notes total − Baseline total, per agent and per dimension.

Design principle

Shared memory should not make separate intelligences look identical. It should let them remain separate while still knowing what changed.
